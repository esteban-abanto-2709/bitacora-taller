# Wishlist

Possible ideas and improvements, no commitment (maybe never). Code `WL-###` (never reused).
If an idea is promoted, it's deleted from here and a new `RM` is born.

**Format:** title + the idea in 1–2 lines, no fields.

---

## [WL-001] Push notifications for due services
Notify the mechanic with the app closed. Needs a scheduled job and web push; on iPhone it only works with the app installed.

## [WL-002] Google sign-in
One tap from the phone instead of email and password. Needs an OAuth client in Google Cloud.

## [WL-003] App icons for the PWA
A logo and the 192/512 px icons so the app installs with its own icon on the home screen.

## [WL-004] Expense reports
Spending per vehicle or per month, built from the optional maintenance cost.

## [WL-005] Delete vehicles
Left out of the MVP on purpose: deleting would take the history with it. Add only if the workshop asks, with confirmation.

## [WL-006] Multi-workshop accounts
Each workshop sees only its own cars, so other workshops could use the same deployment.
